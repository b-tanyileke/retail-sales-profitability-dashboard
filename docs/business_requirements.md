# Business Requirements — Retail Sales & Profitability Intelligence

## 1. Project Overview

**Project:** Retail Sales & Profitability Intelligence Dashboard  
**Role:** Business Intelligence Analyst / Dashboard Developer  
**Business:** Superstore Retail (fictional)  
**Data Source:** Tableau Sample Superstore  
**Project Type:** Business Intelligence / Sales & Profitability Analytics

### Business Problem

Retail management needs a consolidated view of sales performance, profitability, and operational patterns across geographic regions, products, and customer segments.

Revenue performance alone does not adequately indicate business health. High sales volumes may coexist with weak or negative profitability, requiring additional investigation.

### Project Objective

Develop an interactive business intelligence solution that enables management to:

- Monitor revenue, profit, and order performance.
- Identify profitable and underperforming product categories.
- Analyze regional and customer-segment performance.
- Investigate relationships between discounting and profitability.
- Identify opportunities for improving commercial performance.

## 2. Target Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Primary Information Needs |
| --- | --- |
| Sales Director | Revenue trends, geographic performance, sales growth, category performance |
| Finance / Operations Manager | Profit margins, loss-making products, discount exposure, profitability improvement |

## 3. Key Business Questions

**BQ1 — Overall Performance**  
How are sales, profits, and order volumes changing over time?

**BQ2 — Product Profitability**  
Which product categories, subcategories, and products generate the greatest and least profit?

**BQ3 — Geographic Performance**  
Which regions and states contribute the most revenue and profit?

**BQ4 — Discount Effectiveness**  
How do discount levels relate to profitability, and where are discounts associated with financial losses?

**BQ5 — Customer Segmentation**  
Which customer segments contribute the most to sales and profits?

## 4. Key Performance Indicators

| KPI | Business Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Total Sales | Sum of recorded sales revenue |
| Total Profit | Sum of recorded profit |
| Profit Margin (%) | Total Profit / Total Sales × 100 |
| Total Orders | Number of distinct order IDs |
| Average Order Value | Total Sales / Total Orders |
| Units Sold | Sum of quantities sold |
| Sales Growth (%) | Percentage change in sales between comparable periods |
| Loss-Making Sales Share (%) | Percentage of sales generated by order lines recording negative profit |

Metric definitions are provisional until source data verification. Growth comparisons must use equivalent time periods, and all calculations will be validated against SQL results.

## 5. Functional Requirements

### Dashboard A — Executive Performance Overview

The dashboard should provide:

- Key sales, profit, margin, and order KPIs.
- Historical performance trends.
- Revenue and profitability by region.
- Sales and profit breakdowns by category.
- Interactive filtering by relevant dimensions.

### Dashboard B — Profitability & Discount Diagnostics

The dashboard should provide:

- Profitability comparisons across product categories and subcategories.
- Identification of underperforming products.
- Discount and profit relationship analysis.
- Geographic or segment-level profitability investigation.
- Interactive drill-down capabilities where appropriate.

## 6. Technical Requirements

- **Excel:** Initial data inspection and exploratory analysis.
- **SQLite / SQL:** Structured storage, data quality checks, KPI calculations, and business analysis.
- **Python:** Reproducible data loading, processing, and export.
- **Tableau Desktop Public Edition:** Dashboard development.
- **Tableau Public:** Public dashboard hosting.
- **GitHub:** Version control, methodology, source code, and project documentation.

## 7. Data Requirements

The dataset must support:

- Transaction or order-line-level sales analysis.
- Date-based aggregation and comparisons.
- Geographic analysis.
- Product and category analysis.
- Customer-segment analysis.
- Profit and discount analysis.

Data quality checks must cover null values, duplicate records, field types, date validity, and any other material inconsistencies.

## 8. Scope and Limitations

### In Scope

- Descriptive and diagnostic analytics.
- Sales and profitability monitoring.
- Discount and segment comparisons.
- Interactive Tableau dashboards.
- Reproducible SQL analysis.
- Business insight documentation.

### Out of Scope — Initial Release

- Real-time sales monitoring.
- Inventory forecasting.
- Production database deployment.
- Predictive machine-learning models.
- Causal claims about discounts and profitability.

### Limitations

Sample Superstore is a synthetic, historical dataset. Findings demonstrate analytical methodology rather than verified performance of a real business.

Associations between discounting and profitability must not be presented as causal effects.

## 9. Success Criteria

The project is successful when:

1. The source dataset has been verified and documented.
2. Data quality checks and analytical transformations are reproducible.
3. Core Tableau KPI results reconcile with independent SQL calculations.
4. Both dashboards answer their intended business questions.
5. Filters, dashboard actions, and tooltips work correctly.
6. Key findings are supported by data and distinguish observations from recommendations.
7. The published Tableau Public dashboard is usable and visually consistent.
8. The GitHub repository contains setup instructions, code, methodology, screenshots, and links to the finished dashboard.

## 10. Deliverables

- Documented business requirements and data dictionary.
- Reproducible data preparation scripts.
- SQLite schema and analytical SQL queries.
- Cleaned, visualization-ready dataset.
- Tableau workbook containing two interactive dashboards.
- Written findings and recommendations.
- Published Tableau Public dashboard.
- GitHub project README and portfolio entry.
